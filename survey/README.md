# Survey workspace

Placeholder for future authorised survey planning and implementation. No questionnaire, form, publication or response collection has been implemented.

Before starting, confirm the research scope and data-handling requirements with the participant. Publishing a survey or accessing an external account requires explicit approval.

Keep response exports and identifying information outside the repository. If local storage is later authorised, `survey/responses/` and `survey/exports/` are ignored safeguards; neither directory is created by this setup. CSV, TSV, spreadsheet and JSONL exports anywhere under this directory are also ignored. Ignore rules do not inspect content.

Track only reviewed, non-sensitive materials. Use synthetic data for future checks. Follow [AGENTS.md](../AGENTS.md) and record meaningful work in the [development log](../docs/codex-development-log.md).
