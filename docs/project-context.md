# Project context

Last updated: 9 November 2026 (Africa/Johannesburg).

## Purpose and current stage

This exploratory project concerns digital banking confidence, financial technology accessibility and community cybersecurity awareness in South Africa. It is being developed during the Andela × OpenAI Codex Accelerator, Cape Town Cohort 2026.

Current work is limited to a local research repository and development documentation. No survey or application has been implemented in this repository. The final capstone direction has not been selected, and no research findings are claimed.

## Research origin and evidence boundaries

The participant reports observing that some South Africans struggle to use banking applications and ATMs independently. Some seek help from relatives, bank employees or strangers. Potential financial and privacy exposure motivates investigation; this is not a measured finding about prevalence, causes or outcomes.

Use the following distinctions throughout the project:

- **Verified source content:** requirements checked against the supplied capstone brief and onboarding deck, with page or slide references.
- **Participant-provided context:** the observations above, the cohort details and the submission deadline supplied in this conversation.
- **Open research questions:** matters to investigate, without assuming a particular answer.
- **Assumptions:** tentative working choices that must be labelled and revisited when evidence becomes available.

No interviews, response exports, sample sizes, statistics or research outcomes were supplied or analysed during repository setup.

## Research directions supplied by the participant

- Difficulties understanding and using digital banking services.
- Digital confidence and independence.
- Trust and reliance on informal assistance.
- Financial fraud awareness.
- Language and accessibility barriers.
- Whether practical simulations could improve learning.
- Whether community experiences could improve digital safety education.

These are research directions, not established conclusions, selected interventions or AI-generated capstone proposals.

## Capstone scope

The specific **Capstone Brief**, pages 1–2, focuses on technology that helps Africans create, share, discover or protect their own stories. Its four tracks are:

1. Voices & Languages.
2. Discovery & Audience.
3. Creation & Craft.
4. Truth, Authorship & Ownership.

Page 3 calls for a working proof of concept rather than production-ready software and explicitly reserves original capstone idea generation for the participant. Codex must not generate original capstone ideas.

Digital banking research does not automatically qualify for this brief. The participant must select the final direction and establish its fit. See [decision D001](decisions.md#d001-research-and-capstone-selection-remain-separate).
