## Research origin and evidence boundaries

The participant reports observing that some South Africans struggle to use banking applications and ATMs independently. Some seek help from relatives, bank employees or strangers. Potential financial and privacy exposure motivates investigation; this is not a measured finding about prevalence, causes or outcomes.

Use the following distinctions throughout the project:

- **Verified source content:** requirements checked against the supplied capstone brief and onboarding deck, with page or slide references.
- **Participant-provided context:** the observations above, the cohort details and the submission deadline supplied in this conversation.
- **Open research questions:** matters to investigate, without assuming a particular answer.
- **Assumptions:** tentative working choices that must be labelled and revisited when evidence becomes available.

No interviews, response exports, sample sizes, statistics or research outcomes were supplied or analysed during repository setup.

## Privacy and next steps

Preserve respondent privacy from the start. Private responses, interviews, transcripts, recordings and identifying details must not enter Git, development logs or examples. Future validation should use synthetic data. Ignore rules are a safeguard, not a substitute for reviewing content.

Future research must investigate competing explanations, selection and recall biases, and contradictory evidence. Do not treat staff observations, fictional demonstrations or technical test results as representative customer evidence or proof that an intervention works.

The prototype uses fictional data only. Current functionality uses version-bound exact audience approval, moderator decisions, rule-based candidate matching and simulated institutional delivery/response. Corrections and withdrawal stop further sharing without pretending to recall delivered copies. Preserve the approved application and local demo data. No deadline pressure justifies reduced engineering quality, research rigour or honest reporting. External accounts, data access, further pushes and deployment require the applicable explicit approval.
